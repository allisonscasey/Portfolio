Growing up as a competitive dancer, pianist, singer, and actress, people often ask how I ended up pursuing engineering. From a young age, I struggled to put a response into words because to me the connection always made perfect sense. I believe the best engineers are the creative ones who think outside the box to build innovative solutions. Whether developing something completely new, improving an existing system, or meeting tight constraints, engineering at its core is about designing and creating solutions that work in the real world.
